How can we help?

Your games, your progress. Here’s how to keep them with you.

Moving to Google Play

The preview APK and Google Play version use different signing identities. Android may require you to uninstall the preview before installing the Play version. Uninstalling removes its private library, so export a backup first.

  1. Export from the preview

    Open Settings → Export full backup. In the Android share sheet, save the ZIP somewhere outside Koki, such as Downloads, a computer, or your own cloud drive.

  2. Keep a copy

    Check that the ZIP exists in the destination you chose. Keep it until you have restored and checked your games. Do not send ROMs or private backups to support.

  3. Restore in the Play version

    After installing Koki from Google Play, choose Restore backup on the welcome screen or in Settings. Select your exported ZIP and check your library and progress before deleting any copy.

The Google Play version is being prepared. The website’s current APK remains a preview download.

Adding games

Choose Add games and select your own .gba, .bin, or .zip files. Koki copies them into your local library. No games or Nintendo BIOS are included, and Koki does not download commercial ROMs.

Original input test

Try the original 32 KiB Koki diagnostic to check emulation, A-button input, D-pad markers, and an SRAM save marker. It is a test utility, not a full game, and contains no commercial game data or Nintendo BIOS.

Saves and backups

Save inside the game for ordinary .sav progress. Checkpoints restore a complete emulator session and are specific to Koki’s emulator core. Use Game details → Export save to move an in-game save between compatible emulators.

A full Koki backup includes imported games, saves, checkpoints, artwork, and settings. Android exports use the share sheet; choose a destination outside Koki. Export before clearing app storage, reinstalling, or moving phones.

Touch controls

While playing, open the upper-right game menu and choose Edit controls. Drag a control to move it, select it to change its size, or choose a preset. Portrait and landscape layouts are saved separately. Done saves your changes.
