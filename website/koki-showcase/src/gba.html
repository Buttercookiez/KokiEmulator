Questions

All emulators
Are game files included?+

No. Import your own legally obtained GBA files. No commercial ROMs or Nintendo BIOS are bundled with Koki.

Can I move progress from another emulator?+

Koki supports standard .sav files for compatible in-game saves. My Boy! snapshots and other emulator-specific checkpoints use different formats and cannot be imported as Koki checkpoints.

Do I need an account?+

The Koki emulator apps don’t require an account or connection to play. Your imported library and saves stay on your device.

What’s next for Koki?+

iOS and more emulator systems are planned. Link features, cheat management and ROM patching remain on the roadmap and are not active in this preview.
