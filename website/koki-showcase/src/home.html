FAQs

Are games included?+

No. Bring your own compatible game files. Commercial games and console BIOS files aren’t bundled.

Can I play offline?+

Yes. Once installed, Koki runs offline. Your imported library, saves and settings stay on your device.

Which emulators are available?+

Koki GBA is available for Windows and Android. Game Boy Color, Nintendo DS and PSP support are planned, with no release dates announced.
