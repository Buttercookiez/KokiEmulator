## Short description

An offline GBA emulator with customizable controls, saves and a quiet library.

## Full description

Play your own Game Boy Advance games in a clean, personal library. Koki is an offline GBA emulator with local saves, adjustable touch controls and a minimalist interface.

Make the controls yours
Move and resize the on-screen buttons, choose a control preset, and keep separate portrait and landscape layouts. Save a different setup for each game. Compatible keyboards and standard gamepads are also supported.

Keep your progress
Use in-game saves, named checkpoints and automatic resume. Preview a checkpoint before restoring it, keep recovery history, and import or export standard .sav files. Export a full library backup before moving devices or reinstalling.

Play at your pace
Pause, rewind, fast-forward or slow down. Adjust volume, display filters and the game picture position. Optional turbo buttons and touch vibration let you tailor the controls to your device.

A library that feels like yours
Search and sort your games, mark favorites, switch between grid and list, and choose your own covers. Use light, dark or system appearance with saved color choices and reduced-motion support.

Local by design
Koki has no required account, advertisements, in-app purchases or analytics. Imported games, progress, artwork and settings stay on your device. You choose when to export or share a backup.

Bring your own games
Import your own supported .gba, .bin or .zip files. No games or Nintendo BIOS are included. Use only files you have the right to use. Compatibility and performance can vary by game and device.

Koki is an independent emulator and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.
